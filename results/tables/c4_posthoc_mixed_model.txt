=== C4 POST-HOC SENSITIVITY ANALYSIS (non-pre-registered) ===
Primary, pre-registered C4 analysis (chi-squared, independent counts) is in
statistical_tests.txt / 03_analysis.R.

Method: GEE (geepack::geeglm), cluster = variant (each of the 509 eligible
variants contributes one paired observation per model), exchangeable working
correlation, robust (sandwich) standard errors. This directly represents the
repeated-measures pairing across models that a case-level-only random
intercept does not capture.
